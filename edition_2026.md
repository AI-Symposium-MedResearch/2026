## 2026 Recap

Thank you to everyone who joined the **2026 edition** of the _Symposium: AI in Medical Research_ on October 28 at Arminius Church, Rotterdam.

### Highlights

From insightful keynotes by **Geert Litjens, Martijn Bauer, and Esther Bron** to research pitch presentations, we explored the opportunities and challenges of bringing AI into healthcare.

Our speed-dating and poster session brought together researchers, industry representatives and healthcare professionals to share ideas and connect over posters, drinks, and lively conversations!
